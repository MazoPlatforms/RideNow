Design priorities

The basics need to be
clear and dependable.

Three things we are designing toward.

01

Know who you’re travelling with

Trip screens should clearly show relevant Rider, Driver, and vehicle details once a ride is accepted.

02

Location with purpose

Location permission should be explained in context and requested only for defined journey features.

03

A clear way to get help

RideNow must establish and publish support, complaint, and incident-response processes before service launch.

02 / If there is immediate danger

Contact the appropriate local emergency service first.

RideNow has not published an emergency response line or a live safety team. Do not rely on this website for urgent assistance.

03 / Still to be defined

Operations, not just interface.

Safety tools require verified processes and responsible teams before they can be described as available.
