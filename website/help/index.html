Getting started

Can I book a RideNow ride today?

Not yet. The Rider app and service are in development. This website does not accept ride requests.

Can I create a Rider or Driver account?

No. Account registration and authentication are not connected to this website.

Is there a RideNow app listing?

There are no public app-store listings today. The Rider and Driver apps are development previews.

Fares and payments

What will rides cost?

Fare rates have not been set or published. Any future fare and applicable charges should be shown before a Rider confirms a trip.

How is payment being designed?

RideNow is being designed cash-first, with amounts in Sri Lankan rupees (LKR). Electronic payment options have not been announced.

Does RideNow charge Drivers commission?

RideNow’s stated model is zero commission on Driver fares. Other fees, settlement rules, and final Driver terms still need to be confirmed.

Location, safety, and privacy

Where will RideNow operate?

Operational service areas have not been announced. The Colombo imagery on this site is illustrative, not an availability map.

Why might the apps ask for location?

The planned apps may use location to set a pickup point and support availability or an active trip. This website does not request location.

How can I contact support about an account or trip?

There are no live accounts or trips, and a monitored support channel has not been published. Do not send sensitive personal information through this preview.
