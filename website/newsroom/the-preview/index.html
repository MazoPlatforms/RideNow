The short version

Not a live
transport service.

RideNow is an original Sri Lankan ride-hailing project in development. This website shares the direction of its Rider and Driver experiences; it is not a live transport service.

01

What you can explore

The website presents concept designs for the Rider and Driver apps, explains the intended cash-first approach in Sri Lankan rupees, and describes the product principles being considered. Interactive app-store controls are previews only.

02

What is not available

The site does not accept ride requests, create accounts, or take payments. There are no public app-store listings, announced service locations, or published fare rates. SMS verification, live maps, dispatch, route search, payments, and Driver registration are not connected to this website.

03

What happens next

RideNow will publish confirmed product and availability information here when it is ready. No launch date or service coverage is being announced in this note.

More notes, when verified.
