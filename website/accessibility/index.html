Design commitments

Accessibility belongs in
the everyday details.

Four commitments we are designing toward.

01

Readable and adaptable

Use legible type, sufficient contrast, zoom-friendly layouts, and support for system text settings.

02

Keyboard and assistive technology

Use semantic controls, visible focus, clear names, and a logical reading order on the website.

03

Language and context

Plan for English, Sinhala, and Tamil, and explain permissions and trip decisions in plain language.

04

Real-world accessibility

Accessibility needs in vehicles, pickup points, service processes, and support require research and operational planning.

02 / Feedback channel

No monitored contact yet.

The contact form is a visual and validation preview; it cannot deliver feedback. A public feedback route should be provided before launch.
