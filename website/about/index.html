01 / The story so far

A product in progress,
shaped around everyday journeys.

RideNow is an original Sri Lankan ride-hailing project in development. The work today is about building a thoughtful foundation for Riders and Drivers: a clear experience, familiar payments, and useful tools for real journeys. There is no operational ride service to book yet.

02 / Our mission

Make getting there
feel simpler.

Three commitments guiding the work.

01

For the journey

Design around the real steps of a ride, with clear information at each point.

02

For Sri Lanka

Build for local context, Sinhala, Tamil and English, and prices expressed in LKR.

03

For both sides

Give Riders and Drivers an experience that respects their time and choices.

03 / Our vision

A more connected way to move through the places we call home.

That vision will be measured by what the product can reliably deliver, not by promises made before launch.
