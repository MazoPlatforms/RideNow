Privacy, in plain language

Your information.
Clearly explained.

This notice explains the information RideNow may use to plan and provide its Rider and Driver services, and what still needs to be confirmed before those services launch.

Development notice — details need confirmation before launchRideNow is currently an informational website and the Rider and Driver apps are in development. This site does not currently accept ride bookings, create service accounts, collect trip payments, or request device location. Product descriptions below explain planned data uses; they are not a claim that those features are live. The operator's legal identity, privacy contact, providers, retention periods, and some processing details have not yet been supplied and must be confirmed by RideNow and reviewed by Sri Lankan counsel before a live service begins.

1. Service status and scope

This notice covers ridenow.lk and, when released, the RideNow Rider and RideNow Driver apps and related ride-hailing services in Sri Lanka. The website currently presents product concepts only. Its app preview controls are not connected to an account, booking, SMS, payment, or location service.

The current website code has no sign-up or booking form, analytics tag, or tracking-cookie implementation. The site does not intentionally ask for personal information through a form. The hosting and security providers used for the site, and whether their infrastructure records request or diagnostic logs, must be confirmed before publication of a final notice.

2. Who is responsible

“RideNow” is the product name used on this website. The legal entity that will operate the service, its registered or business address, and its privacy contact have not been provided. They must be added here before accounts or rides are offered. This draft does not identify a company or imply that RideNow has a particular registration status.

3. Information that may be involved

If the planned service launches, the information needed will depend on whether someone uses RideNow as a Rider, a Driver, or both. The final notice must identify exact data fields and mandatory or optional uses.

Rider accounts and trips

  • Contact and account details, such as a phone number, name, language preference, and account or verification status.
  • Trip details, such as pickup and drop-off points, route or trip status, timestamps, fare information, and communications needed to arrange or support a ride.
  • Device and service diagnostics, such as app version, device identifiers, security events, crash information, and network information, if the released app or providers collect them.

Driver accounts and service

  • Contact and account information, identity and eligibility documents, licence and vehicle details, and verification results needed for onboarding and lawful operation.
  • Availability, accepted or declined offers, trip records, and location information needed to show availability, match a ride, or support an active trip.

Communications and payments

RideNow is being designed around cash-first payment in Sri Lankan rupees. A cash trip may involve recording a fare or settlement status. If card, wallet, bank, or other electronic payment options are later introduced, the payment provider, data handled, fees, and terms must be disclosed before use. Message content and delivery details may be handled if support, SMS verification, or service notifications are implemented.

4. Location permission

The planned Android apps may use device location to set a pickup point and, for Drivers, to show availability or support an active trip. Android permission prompts are controlled by the device operating system. A user should be able to review or change permissions in device settings; declining location may limit location-based functions, while manual entry may be offered where supported.

Any background or continuous location use must be specifically justified, described in context, and requested using Android's separate permission flow where required. RideNow has not confirmed that such a feature will be used. The final notice must state when location is collected, how often, who can see it, and how long it is retained.

5. How information may be used

For a future live service, information may be used to create and secure accounts; verify Driver eligibility; arrange, dispatch, and record trips; show pickup or trip progress; calculate and explain fares; support cash settlement records; respond to support or safety reports; prevent misuse; maintain service reliability; and meet applicable legal obligations.

RideNow must document the purpose and applicable legal basis for each category of processing before launch. This notice does not invent or preselect a consent, contractual, or other legal basis where the product and legal facts are not yet settled.

6. Sharing and service providers

To provide a ride service, some trip details may need to be shared with the other trip participant. For example, a Driver may need the pickup and destination information for an accepted ride, and a Rider may need the Driver and vehicle details shown for that ride. The exact fields, visibility, and timing must be defined and disclosed in the apps.

RideNow may need providers for hosting, databases, maps, geocoding, routing, SMS, push notifications, support, security, and payment processing if those features are selected. No production provider list or hosting region has been confirmed. Before launch, RideNow must identify its providers, explain their roles, assess transfers outside Sri Lanka, and put appropriate contractual and security arrangements in place.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or through a valid legal process, or where reasonably necessary to address a serious safety or security matter, subject to applicable law.

7. Retention and security

RideNow has not set final retention periods for account, identity, trip, location, support, or diagnostic data. Those periods must be determined by purpose, operational need, and applicable legal record-keeping duties. The final notice should explain deletion, account closure, and any records that must be kept after a request.

Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards must be selected and tested before real personal data is processed. No security measure can be described here as a guarantee. No real user information should be entered into the current concept website.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

People should be able to manage app permissions through their device and review relevant account settings in the released apps. The available data access, correction, objection, erasure, complaint, and other rights—and how to exercise them—depend on the law in force and the final service design.

A working privacy request channel and response process have not been provided. RideNow must publish a monitored contact method and verify the identity and authority of a requester before handling account or trip information. Do not send identity documents or sensitive personal information through this informational website.

9. Sri Lankan privacy and related law

RideNow's planned processing should be assessed under Sri Lankan law, including the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, as amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2025, and the commencement orders and rules in force at the time. The Data Protection Authority's Extraordinary Gazette No. 2498/16 of 22 July 2026 appoints 1 January 2027 for the commencement of section 2, section 3, Part I, and Part III of the Act. Other commencement dates and implementation requirements must be checked against current official materials before launch.

How RideNow, its technology providers, and any payment providers fit the relevant legal roles depends on the final operating and technical arrangements. That assessment, notices and consent flows, cross-border processing, data-subject request handling, and incident response require legal and privacy review. This page is informational and is not legal advice.

Official sources: Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act No. 22 of 2025 · Extraordinary Gazette No. 2498/16 · Data Protection Authority materials.

10. Changes and contact

This notice should be updated when the product, providers, data flows, or applicable law changes. A final version should state its effective date and provide a way to review material changes.

The RideNow operating entity, postal address, privacy contact details, and complaint channel remain to be confirmed. They must be published here before the service asks people to create accounts or share trip and location data.
