A clear start

The terms,
before the ride.

These draft terms describe the expectations being shaped for RideNow Riders and Drivers in Sri Lanka. They do not activate a ride service or replace final terms shown in a released app.

Development terms — legal and business details remain openRideNow's website and apps are in development; there is no public ride-request or Driver registration service today. The legal operator, the platform's role in each ride, eligibility and cancellation rules, fare and tax treatment, service areas, insurance arrangements, complaint route, and dispute venue have not all been confirmed. These points must be settled and reviewed by Sri Lankan counsel before these terms are presented as binding launch terms.

1. What is available today

This website is an informational product preview. It does not accept bookings, register Riders or Drivers, arrange rides, or take payments. Buttons labelled as store previews do not link to live app listings. These draft terms therefore do not form a set of operational booking rules for a ride.

When RideNow launches a service, the final terms and any role-specific rules should be presented in the app before a person creates an account or requests or accepts a ride.

2. Who provides RideNow

RideNow is the product name. The legal entity that will provide or operate the platform, its registered address, and its official service contacts have not been confirmed in the material available for this website. They must be identified in the final terms. The platform's legal relationship with Riders, Drivers, and any transport or payment partners also needs to be clearly defined; this draft does not characterize RideNow as an agent, carrier, employer, or contracting party.

3. Accounts and access

A future account flow may use a phone number and a one-time verification code to confirm access. The released app must state what information is required, how to keep an account secure, and how to recover or close it. A verification screen is not evidence that SMS verification or account authentication is currently connected.

Riders should provide accurate account details and protect their sign-in method. Drivers may be asked to submit identity, licence, vehicle, or other eligibility documents for review. RideNow must define the eligibility criteria, verification process, retention rules, appeal or correction path, and account suspension process before Driver onboarding opens.

4. Rider responsibilities

When ride requests become available, Riders will be expected to provide a usable pickup and destination, review the fare and trip details shown before confirming, be ready at the agreed pickup point, treat Drivers respectfully, and follow applicable law and reasonable safety instructions.

The final terms must explain cancellation, waiting, no-show, passenger and luggage limits, accessibility arrangements, and how to report a trip concern. Those rules and any charges have not been finalized and should not be inferred from this draft.

5. Driver responsibilities

Before accepting paid rides, Drivers must meet applicable Sri Lankan licensing, vehicle, passenger-transport, insurance, road-safety, tax, and other legal requirements that apply to their circumstances. Drivers must keep required permissions and documents current, use a roadworthy vehicle, and provide accurate onboarding information.

Drivers are responsible for safe driving, obeying traffic laws, and treating Riders respectfully. RideNow must confirm the precise onboarding checks, required documents, insurance responsibilities, complaint and incident process, and the legal relationship with Drivers before service launch.

RideNow has described a zero-commission model for Driver fares. The exact meaning of “zero commission,” any other fees or charges, tax handling, settlement records, and how the model will be reflected in Driver agreements must be stated in final Driver terms. This page does not establish a fee schedule.

6. Ride requests and trips

A request, offer, acceptance, cancellation, or trip will only be effective under the flow and status shown in a released app. The app must make clear when a request is sent, when a Driver accepts, what trip details are confirmed, and how either participant can report a problem.

RideNow has not announced operational service areas, availability, response times, or a live dispatch service. Maps, geocoding, routing, and operational providers remain subject to selection and business decisions. Map information can be incomplete or delayed, so Drivers must use their judgment and comply with road rules.

7. Fares and payment

RideNow is being designed as a cash-first service with amounts in Sri Lankan rupees (LKR). Final fare calculation, estimates, taxes, tolls, waiting or cancellation charges, fare adjustments, and cash settlement procedures are not yet defined. The fare and applicable charges must be clearly disclosed before a Rider confirms a trip.

No card, wallet, bank transfer, or other electronic payment is currently offered through this website. If any electronic method is added, the provider, payment terms, fees, refunds, and applicable payment requirements must be explained before use. Relevant Sri Lankan rules—including those concerning consumer services and regulated payment activity where applicable—require assessment against RideNow's final business and payment model.

8. Location and notifications

The planned Android apps may request permission for location to set a pickup, show a Driver's availability, or support an active trip. Permission is controlled in device settings and some location-based functions may not work when permission is denied. Any use beyond the active screen, including background tracking, must be separately justified, explained, and handled through Android's permission flow where required.

Push notifications or SMS may be used for service updates if those channels are implemented. Permission to receive device notifications is managed through the operating system. RideNow has not confirmed a production SMS, push, or mapping provider.

9. Safety and acceptable use

Users must not use a released service to break the law, impersonate another person, submit false documents, interfere with the app, or threaten or harass another person. Suspected immediate danger should be reported to the appropriate local emergency service; RideNow has not yet published a dedicated emergency response channel.

Before launch, RideNow must publish clear processes for safety reports, lost property, complaints, account restrictions, service interruptions, and urgent incidents. Nothing in these draft terms limits a right or remedy that cannot lawfully be limited.

10. Sri Lankan law and complaints

RideNow expects to operate in Sri Lanka and its final documents should be reviewed against applicable Sri Lankan law. Relevant frameworks may include the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No. 9 of 2003, the Electronic Transactions Act, No. 19 of 2006, privacy and data-protection requirements, road-traffic and licensing rules, and payment regulation if electronic payment services are introduced. Which provisions apply depends on the final service structure and must be confirmed by counsel.

This draft does not select a court, arbitration process, complaint address, or governing-law clause. Final terms must provide a workable complaint process and explain dispute handling without removing any non-excludable statutory rights. This page is information for product review, not legal advice.

Official sources: Consumer Affairs Authority Act No. 9 of 2003 · Electronic Transactions Act No. 19 of 2006 · Central Bank of Sri Lanka legislative enactments.

11. Changes and contact

Final launch terms should state when they take effect, how users will be told about material changes, and what happens if someone does not accept an update. A version history and accepted-term record may be needed in the apps.

The RideNow operating entity, legal address, customer support and complaints channels, and dispute process remain to be confirmed. These details must be published before the platform offers rides.
