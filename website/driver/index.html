A few things to know

Before you set off.

RideNow is still taking shape. Here’s what’s true today.

Can I start driving with RideNow today?

Not yet. The Driver app and onboarding experience are being developed. There is no public registration flow or service coverage to announce.

How does zero commission work?

RideNow's stated model is to take zero commission from driver fares. The wider operating policies are still being defined.

Where will RideNow operate?

Service areas have not been announced. Operational coverage and provider decisions are still pending.

Is the Driver app available in app stores?

No. The buttons above are informational previews; there is no public Google Play listing or iOS app currently available.
