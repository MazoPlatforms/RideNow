RideNow is in development. Here’s what’s true today.

Can I download RideNow today?

Not yet. The Rider app is in development and there are no public store listings. The buttons above are preview states only.

Is RideNow available in my area?

Service areas have not been announced. Maps, routing, and operating coverage still need explicit provider and business decisions.

How are payments being designed?

RideNow is being designed cash-first, with fares in Sri Lankan rupees (LKR). Final fare rules and any additional payment methods are not yet defined.

Is there an iOS app?

No. An iOS app is not currently available, and there is no App Store listing. The App Store button is an informational preview only.

When will RideNow launch?

There is no launch date or operational service coverage to announce. Follow this page for updates as the product takes shape.
