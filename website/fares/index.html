Pricing principles

What we believe a Rider
should be able to see.

Three things we are designing toward, before any real price exists.

01

A price in LKR

Display trip prices in Sri Lankan rupees, with the currency identified clearly.

02

Details before confirmation

Show the fare and any applicable charges before the Rider confirms a trip.

03

A familiar way to pay

Cash-first is the current design direction. No electronic payment option has been announced.

02 / No rates published

No price list yet.

No fare calculator or price list is available yet. Don’t use this page to estimate a real trip.

Still needs business decisions

  • Vehicle categories
  • Minimum fares
  • Distance or time rates
  • Waiting charges
  • Cancellations
  • Taxes
  • Tolls
  • Settlement rules

03 / Driver model

Zero commission on Driver fares is the stated direction.

The exact settlement process, any other fees, taxes, and Driver agreement terms must be defined and shown before onboarding. No final fare or payout schedule is being represented here.
